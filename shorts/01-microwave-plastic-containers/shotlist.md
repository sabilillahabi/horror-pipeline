# Shotlist: Is It Safe to Microwave Plastic Containers? (stop-motion rebuild)

Stop-motion rebuild of Short #1 with the STOPMOTION.md method. Same
script and sources as `script.md` (re-timed 2026-11-25, ~41 s, 10 lines).
Location: the same bright daytime kitchen as the stop-motion test. The
location reference is the test keyframe
(`tests/stop-motion/frames/F01.jpg`), attached to every generation along
with the 4 model sheets.

Beat order per SHORTS_FORMAT.md: Hook, Foreshadow, Short answer, Why,
Solution (lines 4-6), Bonus fact, Rescue, Loop. The Solution beat spans
lines 4-6: line 4 (look for the label), line 5 (no label means nobody
tested it), line 6 (toss the warped container, the Foreshadow payoff).
Waffle's peril stays cartoon slapstick only (startle, fur up, sheepish),
never real pain or distress.

Drawing budget: 30 unique drawings in the v2 cut (target 30-36). Holds and the 2-3
drawing mouth/expression cycles cover the rest of the ~41 s. The Loop
shot reuses shot 1's keyframe. v2 (2026-11-26, per the new SHORTS_FORMAT.md
framing rule): 3 new close-up keyframes (K8 Noir's face, K9 Waffle's
reaction, K10 the tub warping) plus hub-and-spoke edits; 7 old drawings
superseded by the close-ups (K2c, K4b, K4c, K4d, K5d, K6c, K7c) — files
kept locally, not in the v2 schedule. K8 and K9 are reused across shots
(same drawing, counts once). Framing changes at least every 5 s: close-up
or angle change at slots 3 (K1b), 16 (K8), 28 (K3c), 35 (K10), 41 (K9),
58 (K8), 75 (K5f top-down), 90 (K9), 104 (K8c).

Tail positions: A = swaying left, B = swaying right, C = curled up.
Noir must visibly act in every shot. On-image text: only small comic
SFX words that match an SFX (BEEP in shot 4, POP in shot 5). The
microwave-safe symbol is a simple mark with wavy lines, never readable
words or letters.
