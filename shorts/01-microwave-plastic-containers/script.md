## Sources

- Line 2 (only if it says microwave-safe): USDA FSIS, "Microwave Ovens and Food Safety" — "Only use cookware that is specially manufactured for use in the microwave oven. Glass, ceramic containers, and all plastics that are safe to use usually will be labeled for microwave oven use." FDA, "Microwave Ovens" — "Use microwave safe cookware specially manufactured for use in the microwave oven."
- Line 3 (tub made for cold food, heat warps it, chemicals migrate): USDA FSIS, "Cooking Safely in the Microwave Oven" — "Plastic storage containers such as margarine tubs, take-out containers, whipped topping bowls, and other one-time use containers should not be used in microwave ovens. These containers can warp or melt, possibly causing harmful chemicals to migrate into the food." USDA FSIS, "Microwave Ovens and Food Safety" — cold storage containers (margarine tubs, cottage cheese and yogurt cartons) "are not approved for cooking and chemicals can migrate into food."
- Line 4 (look for the label, made for microwave heat): USDA FSIS, "Microwave Ovens and Food Safety" — "Only use cookware that is specially manufactured for use in the microwave oven." REWRITTEN from Bil's draft ("It means the container was tested for microwave heat") because no current official FDA/USDA page states the label means the container was tested. "Made for microwave heat" is the direct paraphrase of the USDA wording.
- Line 5 (no label doesn't mean poison, nobody tested it): comedic paraphrase of the USDA rule. No separate factual claim. The USDA rule is that only cookware specially manufactured for microwave use (and labeled as such) is designated for the microwave, so an unlabeled container carries no manufacturer assurance.
- Line 6 (warped or melted, toss it): USDA FSIS, "Microwave Ovens and Food Safety" — "Food cooked in any container or packaging that has warped or melted during heating" is listed under NOT SAFE TO USE. REWRITTEN from Bil's draft ("Scratched or cracked? Toss it. Damage lets more stuff leak out.") because no current official FDA/USDA page backs the scratched/cracked claim. The warped/melted wording is the backed version.
- Line 7 (takeout boxes, butter tubs, water bottles): USDA FSIS, "Cooking Safely in the Microwave Oven" — "Plastic storage containers such as margarine tubs, take-out containers, whipped topping bowls, and other one-time use containers should not be used in microwave ovens." Butter tubs are margarine tubs; water bottles are one-time use containers.
- Line 8 (glass or ceramic): USDA FSIS, "Microwave Ovens and Food Safety" — SAFE TO USE: "Heatproof glass (such as Pyrex, Anchor Hocking, etc.)" and "Glass-ceramic (such as Corning Ware)."
