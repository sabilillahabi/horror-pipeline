# Metadata: Is It Safe to Microwave Plastic Containers?

## Title

Is It Safe to Microwave Plastic Containers?

## Description

Short answer: only if it says microwave-safe. Part of the Is It Actually Safe to...? series. Sources: USDA FSIS, "Microwave Ovens and Food Safety" (https://permanent.fdlp.gov/websites/fsisusdagov/www.fsis.usda.gov/wps/wcm/connect/b1539e27-362d-4a32-84f4-334aa0870b7a/Microwave_Ovens_and_Food_Safety.pdf-MOD=AJPERES.pdf); USDA FSIS, "Cooking Safely in the Microwave Oven" (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-A110-PURL-gpo127402/pdf/GOVPUB-A110-PURL-gpo127402.pdf); FDA, "Microwave Ovens" (https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/resources-you-radiation-emitting-products/microwave-ovens).

## Sources

- USDA FSIS, "Microwave Ovens and Food Safety" — only use cookware specially manufactured for microwave use; safe plastics are labeled; cold storage containers (margarine tubs, cottage cheese and yogurt cartons) are not approved for cooking and chemicals can migrate into food; heatproof glass and glass-ceramic are safe to use; food cooked in warped or melted packaging is not safe. https://permanent.fdlp.gov/websites/fsisusdagov/www.fsis.usda.gov/wps/wcm/connect/b1539e27-362d-4a32-84f4-334aa0870b7a/Microwave_Ovens_and_Food_Safety.pdf-MOD=AJPERES.pdf
- USDA FSIS, "Cooking Safely in the Microwave Oven" — plastic storage containers such as margarine tubs, take-out containers, whipped topping bowls, and other one-time use containers should not be used in microwave ovens; they can warp or melt, possibly causing harmful chemicals to migrate into the food. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-A110-PURL-gpo127402/pdf/GOVPUB-A110-PURL-gpo127402.pdf
- FDA, "Microwave Ovens" — some plastic containers should not be used in a microwave oven because they can be melted by the heat of the food inside; use microwave safe cookware specially manufactured for use in the microwave oven. https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/resources-you-radiation-emitting-products/microwave-ovens
